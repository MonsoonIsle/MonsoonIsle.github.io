At a glance

The Arrow is intended for users aged 13 and over and needs no player account. Progress and settings are saved on your device.

Before playing, read the complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Use and choose Accept. Your saved acceptance covers the ordinary Firebase Analytics/GA4 and GameAnalytics gameplay statistics and Firebase Crashlytics Java/NDK reports described below. Advertising choices are handled separately by Google UMP. Declining advertising purposes does not cancel ordinary acceptance.

Clearing local data resets progress but does not automatically delete information already received by Google, GameAnalytics or Unity. For privacy questions or requests, email aquark314@gmail.com.

2. Local and provided information

Game records on your device

The game saves settings, level progress, achievements, daily rewards, unfinished games and diagnostic records on your device to restore game state and run these features. After valid acceptance and the applicable recipient, age, storage and SDK checks, local analytics records can contain gameplay events and randomly generated installation, session and event identifiers. Our pending ordinary events are bounded to three days. Temporary permission checks preserve previously eligible pending data; a new recipient or retired ordinary scope does not inherit that data. This version does not use the earlier default-on restricted-measurement scheme.

Local storage does not mean all related information stays on your device. Some progress, gameplay and advertising events are sent through the analytics service in section 3. This differs from uploading complete saved games or providing cloud saves.

Information you provide

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Please provide only necessary information and avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

The game needs no player account and does not ask for your name or contact details. It currently does not request access to precise device location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

Legal documents included with the app

The complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are included in the installed app and can be read offline. The consent screen does not check for or download document updates. Revised documents are delivered with app updates through Google Play. Opening a website link yourself can send ordinary network request metadata, including your IP address, to that website.

If you choose Share ad diagnostics, the app creates a small local status file and opens the Android share sheet. This basic file excludes installation/session/advertising identifiers and raw ad-response data. No recipient is selected and no file is automatically uploaded. Export files remain in app cache; on a later export the app removes older export files and limits their number. Clearing app cache removes these local export copies.

3. Analytics, promotion and crash reports

GameAnalytics receives gameplay, technical and randomly generated identifier data under the explicit saved acceptance for the recipient scope that includes GameAnalytics. It provides gameplay dashboards alongside Firebase. We configure it to use our random installation identifier and avoid advertising identifiers. Advertising event delivery also follows the separate advertising-measurement gate. Temporary ordinary-permission checks pause submission; changes to the ordinary recipient scope retire our own pending queue. If the initialized SDK identity changes, GA delivery waits for a restart. SDK-held or already transmitted data is not guaranteed to be erased by a local change. GameAnalytics retention and deletion follow its applicable service configuration and policies independently of Firebase retention settings.

The Arrow uses Google Analytics for Firebase/GA4 for ordinary product statistics after your explicit Privacy and Terms acceptance and the applicable technical and age checks. Events can include gameplay and ad activity; launches, session duration, level starts/results, progress, tutorials, hints and rewards; loading times and technical errors; event times, app version, device model, operating system and language; and random installation, session and event identifiers. Google can also process SDK app-instance identifiers and approximate geographic information. These identifiers can link events, so the information is not necessarily anonymous.

Advertising measurement is separate. Firebase advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization follow their own actual UMP signals. Our custom ad-revenue and ad-identity records additionally require the relevant advertising-measurement purposes and the original advertising generation. Unknown or refused advertising purposes are not inferred from Accept or from permission to request an ad. A later advertising choice does not relabel old ad records or reset eligible ordinary gameplay statistics.

Crash reports

The same acceptance explicitly covers Firebase Crashlytics Java/NDK reports to investigate crashes, freezes and technical errors. Reports can include crash traces, native crash data, device and operating-system details, app version, diagnostic identifiers and, when analytics is enabled, preceding analytics events. We do not add your name, email address or complete saved game to crash reports. Automatic report upload remains disabled: eligible previous-session reports may be submitted on a later launch under the same accepted recipient scope. A temporary permission check retains eligible reports; a different recipient scope requires separate SDK preparation. Already submitted or in-transit reports cannot be recalled by a local change.

Older optional analytics/crash switches and UMP publisher content/development purposes do not generate the new acceptance. This version has no separate ordinary-statistics or crash-sharing switch. Reading the documents, pressing Back, changing language or choosing an ad does not create acceptance.

4. Advertising

The Arrow uses Google AdMob mediation for rewarded, interstitial and banner ads, with Google and Unity Ads as advertising providers. You can choose to watch rewarded ads for an in-game benefit.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes IP addresses and inferred approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics such as performance information, and device or account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID. Processing depends on SDK version, configuration, device settings and applicable consent status. App set ID serves permitted purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention, not ad personalization.

Ad personalization depends on applicable consent, advertising configuration and device settings. Non-personalized ads can still involve data for delivery, frequency control, security and measurement. Watching a rewarded ad does not itself mean consenting to unrelated tracking or personalized advertising.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Section 8 describes your available choices.

Unity Ads receives information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, which can include device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Personalized advertising depends on applicable advertising choices and SDK configuration. Unity is an advertising recipient, not an additional recipient of our ordinary gameplay analytics. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

We pass available advertising consent and US opt-out signals to Unity separately from Accept. Missing or incomplete signals do not grant personalized advertising permission. Changing advertising choices stops new ad requests while the choice is being processed and discards our cached ads; it cannot recall data already sent or erase all data held by an SDK or provider.

7. Retention and deletion

We use Google and GameAnalytics services and do not separately store raw gameplay analytics on servers we operate ourselves. Records on your device, provider-hosted data and support emails you send us remain subject to the following separate retention rules:

• Local saves and analytics records: the game saves progress, settings and local analytics records on your device until updated, overwritten or removed. Clear The Arrow's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove local data; this resets progress. Migration or backup services may keep separate copies that you need to manage through them.

• Firebase Analytics data: Google hosts this data. Our retention policy for applicable user-level and event-level analytics data is 2 months, with expired data removed through Google's scheduled deletion process. This period does not apply to standard aggregate reports, AdMob advertising data or local device records. See Google Analytics data retention.

• Firebase Crashlytics data: Google retains crash traces, extracted native crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Native minidumps are held temporarily for processing. This is separate from the Analytics two-month setting. See Firebase privacy and security information.

• AdMob advertising data: Google retains this for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, under applicable policies and legal requirements. Periods can differ by data type and do not follow the Analytics retention setting. See Google's data retention information.

• Unity Ads: advertising information is retained according to Unity’s player privacy policy and applicable purposes and legal requirements, independently of our Firebase Analytics retention setting. Use Unity’s privacy controls and request channels linked in section 4 for information it controls.

• Website hosting logs: GitHub retains these under its hosting service purposes, privacy statement and applicable law.

• Support correspondence: we keep it only as needed to resolve issues, follow up on requests and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it when no longer needed.

Clearing app storage, uninstalling the game or deleting an advertising ID does not automatically erase information already sent to Google, GameAnalytics or Unity. For access to or deletion of information we can identify and control, contact us under section 8. We will explain any legal need to retain information and relevant limitations.

9. Children and younger users

The Arrow is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Store content ratings (such as "3+") describe content suitability and are separate from the game's intended audience and privacy consent requirements.

Users aged 13 and over may still be minors protected by local law. Higher age thresholds or other requirements for data processing, parental consent or personalized advertising continue to apply where required. Reaching age 13 or meeting a store content rating does not replace required consent. Parents and guardians should consider this policy when managing a minor's use.

The game includes third-party ads and analytics that may process the technical information in sections 3 and 4. We do not ask for names or contact details in the game, but this does not mean that no personal information about minors is processed.

If you believe information about a child under 13 has been collected through the game, or a minor's information has been processed without legally required consent or protections, contact aquark314@gmail.com. Once verified, we will delete relevant information we can identify and control, restrict the related processing and help direct requests to service providers where needed.
