At a glance

Your complete saved puzzles and preferences stay on your device. Optional usage events can be sent to Google Analytics for Firebase and to GameAnalytics only while usage statistics are enabled.

In builds with Usage Statistics, sharing starts off. If you turn it on, Google Analytics for Firebase and GameAnalytics receive usage information and Firebase Crashlytics can receive crash reports.

We receive information you choose to send by email. External websites and platform services process information under their own notices.

Privacy and support: mengjingchanyu@gmail.com.

1. Scope and responsibility

Monsoon Isle ("we", "us" or "our") develops Dramadoku and is responsible for the personal information we handle for the purposes described here. This policy covers the current Android game, including its test releases (package: com.monsoonisle.dramadoku), support correspondence and this policy website.

This notice covers builds that offer optional statistics. Earlier offline builds keep gameplay on the device and include no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

2. Local game data

The game stores puzzle progress, character placements, notes, hint usage, elapsed time, completion results and best grades on your device. It also remembers selected chapters, difficulty preferences, tutorial progress and settings such as sound, vibration, reduced motion and display preferences. These records allow you to resume play and keep your choices.

We do not upload complete saved puzzles, placements, notes or answers. When you enable statistics, selected facts about play described below are sent instead. There is no game account, social login or player chat, and we do not ask for your name or email in gameplay.

3. Optional usage statistics and permissions

When available and enabled by you, Google Analytics for Firebase receives page visits, case starts and resumes, completions and ranks, cleared boards, applied hints, wrong submissions, leaving a case, tutorial steps, app start-up time, save or load failures, a count of completed cases and selected settings actions. Event details include the case ID and revision, chapter, difficulty, board size, attempt number, active play seconds, the case-wide hint count and simple counts of undo, note and mark use. GameAnalytics receives the same kinds of play facts as a second analytics service, with its own random installation identifier. Firebase Crashlytics also receives crash and stability data, such as the app version, device model, operating-system version, a stack trace and an installation identifier, when the app fails. Events do not include puzzle answers, full saves, free-text messages, email addresses or a developer-assigned user ID.

Firebase also processes an app-instance identifier and technical information such as app and operating-system versions, device and installation-referral information, sessions and engagement; Google may derive approximate location from network information. An installation identifier can link activity from the same app instance, so this information is not described as anonymous. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Ltd) additionally receives these play facts: page visits, case starts, completions with active play seconds and attempt number, wrong submissions and applied hints for a case ID, leaving a case, tool use, tutorial steps, chapter, difficulty and board-size choices, check-in and shop screen visits, start-up time, the count of completed cases, and failure categories. It also processes a random installation identifier created by this game (not your advertising ID and not an account), session times, the app version, device model, manufacturer, Android version, your IP address and the approximate country derived from it. Its summary segments are coarse (for example a band of completed cases). See https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice. Advertising ID collection and advertising uses of these optional statistics are disabled.

Sharing statistics is optional. Turn it off in Settings → More Settings → Usage Statistics. The game then stops accepting new events and asks Firebase to disable Analytics and Crashlytics collection, delete unsent crash reports, reset local Analytics data, and deletes the unsent GameAnalytics queue and its installation identifier on this device. Events refused while sharing is off are not replayed when you turn it on; instead one summary of how many cases you have completed is sent. Data already transmitted or in transit cannot be recalled by the switch.

The Android integration uses internet, network-state, wake-lock and Play install-referrer permissions for optional analytics, plus vibration for optional touch feedback. It does not request precise-location, contacts, camera, microphone or shared-storage access. You can keep playing offline or with statistics off. Android and Google Play may separately process installation, security and diagnostic information under their own settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until they are replaced or removed. You can delete them through Android's app settings by clearing Dramadoku's storage or uninstalling the game. Clearing storage resets your progress and preferences. The current app disables Android automatic app-data backup and provides no developer-operated cloud backup or remote recovery.

The Google Analytics property is set to retain applicable user-level and event-level data for two months, with no reset on new activity. Aggregate reports have different retention rules. Google retains Crashlytics stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. GameAnalytics stores data under its own retention rules. Turning off sharing stops future collection and does not delete records already received. Contact us about records associated with your installation.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on requests, protect legal rights and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it when no longer needed. You can ask us to delete correspondence we can identify and control. If a legal requirement prevents deletion, we will explain the relevant limitation.

Uninstalling the game does not delete emails you already sent or records held by hosting, email or platform providers. Those providers retain information under their own policies. There is no Dramadoku account to delete.

The game stores up to eight known Analytics app-instance IDs locally, outside Android automatic backup, so you can copy them for a request after turning statistics off. Clearing app data or reinstalling can lose them. These support IDs are not added to gameplay event parameters.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Rights, exceptions and applicable time limits depend on local law.

Send requests to mengjingchanyu@gmail.com with the subject "Dramadoku Privacy Request". For optional statistics, use Settings → More Settings → Usage Statistics → Copy privacy request details, review the text, and include it in your email. Copying does not send it automatically. If an old installation ID is missing, we may be unable to locate its records. We may ask for limited additional information to verify a request and will explain limitations under applicable law.

Because local game progress is not sent to us, we cannot retrieve or erase it remotely; use your device's app-storage controls. For information independently controlled by Google or GitHub, their privacy notices provide their own request channels.

8. Children and younger users

Dramadoku is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. This is our intended audience, not a claim that a rating authority has assigned a particular age rating. Store content ratings may differ by region.

Players aged 13 and over may still be minors under local law. This version does not ask for a date of birth. Where your parent or guardian needs to authorize optional sharing, obtain that permission before turning statistics on. You can continue playing with sharing off. If a parent or guardian has a concern, contact mengjingchanyu@gmail.com so we can investigate and handle identifiable information as required.

9. Information security

Local saves rely on your device's and operating system's access protections. We take reasonable precautions appropriate to the support information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in messages to us.

10. Website visits and external services

This static Monsoon Isle website contains no visitor analytics scripts or advertising, sets no tracking cookies and does not use browser local storage. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Its handling of visit information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Opening a website, sending email or using Google Play involves the relevant browser, email or platform service. Their own privacy policies apply to those interactions. A link to an external service does not give that service access to your local Dramadoku saved games.

Commercial release plans and your choices

In Android builds with ad privacy choices, after you accept the terms, the game asks whether you are 18 or older. You can decline to answer. The game stores your age group on your device. If you say you are 18 or older, the game contacts Google’s User Messaging Platform to check which privacy message applies. Google receives the request, including your IP address, and the form can store your choices on your device. Where available, you can revisit the form in Settings → More Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you are under 18 or skip the question, the game skips this request. You can play with any of these choices.

We plan to use Google AdMob for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads for hints. When enabled, ad partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions, interactions and technical data to select and measure ads, limit repeated ads and prevent fraud. Personalized ads can use activity to infer interests. We plan to offer them to eligible adults where local rules and their choices allow. For minors and players of unknown age, our plan disables personalized ads and individual campaign conversion reporting.

Where required, the game will show the applicable consent form and partner list before requesting ads and provide a way to revisit your choices. If you decline or withdraw personalization, only contextual or limited ads may be requested where permitted. Otherwise the game will skip the ad. Watching a rewarded ad does not enable statistics or authorize personalized ads.

We plan to promote Dramadoku through AppLovin Ads at launch in selected countries. Our initial goal is to measure how many installations our campaigns generate and the cost per install. Before enabling installation measurement, we will identify the mobile measurement partner, describe the information shared with that partner and AppLovin, and explain the choices available to you. We will obtain any required consent before sharing data for this purpose.

Where Remove Ads is available, Google Play handles the one-time payment. Dramadoku uses the product ID, signed purchase information, purchase token and status to check ownership on your device, grant or restore the entitlement and handle revoked purchases. Monsoon Isle does not receive your full payment-card details. Google Play keeps its own transaction records. Removing app data does not delete those records or request a refund. The Terms of Use explain the entitlement.

These plans do not change your installed version or your existing choices. We will update this policy for the actual providers and processing before new services begin. For privacy and deletion requests, email mengjingchanyu@gmail.com.
