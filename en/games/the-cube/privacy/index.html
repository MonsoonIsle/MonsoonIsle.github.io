At a glance

  • The Cube can be played offline without an account. Progress and game settings are saved on your device.
  • Usage statistics are optional and off by default. You can change this choice in the game's settings.
  • Normal gameplay currently does not request or display ads. Development builds have a separate demonstration-ad test screen.
  • Turning statistics off stops future Analytics collection and resets local Analytics data; it does not erase reports already received by Google.
  • For privacy questions, email aquark314@gmail.com.

1. Scope and contact

Monsoon Isle ("we", "us" or "our") develops The Cube: Chain Reaction. This draft covers the current Android development build (package: com.tesselox.game), related support correspondence and visits to this website. It does not replace the separate policy for The Arrow.

Device identifiers, app-instance identifiers and IP addresses may be personal information even when a service does not ask for your name.

Developer and privacy contact: Monsoon Isle, aquark314@gmail.com.

2. Local data and support

The game stores progress, scores, unfinished games and preferences on your device. It does not upload complete saved-game files to a developer-operated server or provide cloud saves. Android backup, migration and store services may have their own data practices.

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Optional usage analytics

The Android app includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Collection is off by default. If you enable Share usage statistics, Google's SDK can process app usage, app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and related technical information described in Firebase's privacy documentation. This information helps us understand use and investigate problems.

The current integration does not supply your name, email address, saved-game file or a custom user ID to Analytics. It disables Analytics advertising-ID collection and automatic screen reporting. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user data and ad-personalization consent remain denied independently of the statistics switch.

The development diagnostics screen can explicitly send an event named sdk_smoke_test, without custom parameters, after statistics have been enabled. Production gameplay-event definitions remain to be confirmed.

Turning statistics off stops future collection and resets Analytics data on the device. That action does not automatically remove reports or records Google has already received.

4. Advertising

Normal gameplay in the current build does not request or display advertisements. Only development builds expose a separate diagnostics screen that can load Google's official demonstration interstitial ads. These are not the developer's production ad units, but they still contact Google's services.

Ad testing requires confirmation by an adult test operator for that session and an update through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Required privacy forms are shown before the SDK permits an ad request. A failed privacy update does not allow an ad request, including when an earlier choice was cached. Advertising privacy choices can be revisited from settings when available.

During consent and demonstration-ad requests, Google may process device and network information, identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics, depending on the SDK, settings and consent. AdMob's processing is separate from optional Firebase Analytics; disabling Analytics does not by itself disable an explicitly requested test ad.

Before release: Confirm the actual ad formats, placements, age treatment, provider data handling and regional consent controls, then update this section. The test-operator confirmation is not a player age-verification system.

5. Purposes and consent

Local data supports gameplay and restoration of progress. Optional analytics supports understanding and improving the game. Development advertising requests validate the integration. Support information is used to answer messages and handle requests.

Consent-dependent processing requires a valid choice. Reading a policy, continuing to play or enabling statistics does not give blanket consent to unrelated advertising or tracking. Declining optional statistics does not prevent offline play.

Before release: Confirm the applicable legal bases, notices and consent requirements for the final audience and launch regions. This draft does not claim those release checks are complete.

6. Service providers

Google receives information through the enabled Analytics service and through explicitly requested consent/ad tests. GitHub Pages hosts this website, and email providers process support correspondence. These services may process information outside your country under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's partner-app data explanation and Firebase privacy information. The final policy must describe any additional providers or processing introduced before release.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until updated or removed, including by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to Android backup and migration behavior. Clearing game storage resets progress. Turning off statistics resets local Analytics data without deleting saved-game progress.

Analytics retention is awaiting console verification. The applicable duration and reset-on-new-activity setting for The Cube's Firebase/Google Analytics property must be recorded here before publication. The Arrow's retention statement is not evidence of The Cube's settings.

Analytics settings do not define retention for AdMob data, standard aggregate reports, hosting logs or support correspondence. Service-provider notices describe their separate processing. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Deleting local data or uninstalling the game does not automatically erase data already sent to a provider. There is no game account to delete. Without accounts or custom user IDs, an email address alone may not identify a particular app installation or Analytics record.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Open Settings → More Settings → Set to read the local privacy summary, turn optional usage statistics on or off, and update or review available advertising privacy choices. Network failures leave offline gameplay available.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact aquark314@gmail.com with the subject "The Cube Privacy Request" and relevant context.

We may need additional information to locate records or verify a request. We will explain limitations where we cannot identify data or a provider independently controls it. Please do not send passwords or identity documents unsolicited.

Before publication, the declared target audience, marketing, advertising and data handling must match the actual product. The store's content rating is a separate questionnaire-based assessment. Age-related protections and consent requirements must be checked for the selected release regions.

10. Security

Local data relies on device and operating-system protections. Google's relevant services use encrypted network connections. No transmission or storage method guarantees absolute security. Protect your device and share only necessary information when contacting support.

11. Website and external links

External websites and services apply their own privacy policies. Their notices do not replace our responsibility to describe the game's actual practices.
