At a glance

  • The Cube can be played offline without an account. Progress and game settings are saved on your device.
  • Usage statistics and crash-report sharing are separate, optional choices, both off by default.
  • The game uses interstitial ads at specified game boundaries and optional rewarded ads for revives. It does not use banner ads.
  • You can change privacy choices in settings. Turning a service off does not erase information already received by Google.
  • For privacy questions, email aquark314@gmail.com.

2. Local data and support

The game stores progress, scores, unfinished games and preferences on your device. It does not upload complete saved-game files to a developer-operated server or provide cloud saves. Android backup, migration and store services may have their own data practices.

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Optional usage analytics

The Android app includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Collection is off by default. If you enable Share usage statistics, Google's SDK can process app usage, app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and related technical information described in Firebase's privacy documentation. We use this information to understand game use, difficulty, revives and ad reliability.

The current integration does not supply your name, email address, saved-game file or a custom user ID to Analytics. It disables Analytics advertising-ID collection and automatic screen reporting. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user data and ad-personalization consent remain denied independently of the statistics switch.

Gameplay events cover starts and ends (game_start, game_end), challenge completions (level_complete), revives (revive_used) and ad requests and outcomes (game_ad_request, game_ad_impression, game_ad_reward, game_ad_failure). Depending on the event, they include the app version, game mode, level, score, revive count and a randomly generated game-attempt identifier, plus the relevant outcome or ad format. The attempt identifier relates events within a game attempt; it is not an account or a developer-assigned persistent user ID. Google may also collect its documented automatic Analytics events.

A development-only diagnostics action can send a parameter-free sdk_smoke_test event after statistics have been enabled.

Turning statistics off stops future collection and resets Analytics data on the device. That action does not automatically remove reports or records Google has already received.

4. Optional crash diagnostics

The Android app includes Firebase Crashlytics for Java and native (NDK) crash diagnostics. Share crash reports is a separate choice and is off by default. It helps us investigate crashes and stability problems; it does not automatically capture every game-script error.

Reports can include crash traces, app version, device and operating-system information, installation/session identifiers and native crash data. If usage statistics are also enabled, Analytics events may appear as diagnostic breadcrumbs. We do not intentionally attach saved games, names, email addresses or a custom user ID.

The SDK can keep reports locally while sharing is disabled. Turning sharing off disables automatic uploads for the next app launch and requests deletion of unsent reports. Restart the app to fully apply this change. An upload already in progress or information already received by Google cannot be recalled by this switch.

See Firebase's crash-data description and Crashlytics' collection controls.

5. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob. Ads need a network connection and may be unavailable. There are no banner ads. The gameplay placements are:

  • Endless: At most one interstitial ad at an ordinary game-over. A final game-over within 60 seconds after a successful rewarded revive skips that interstitial.
  • Challenges: One interstitial opportunity after every three first-time level completions. Replaying a previously completed level does not advance this count.
  • Optional revives: In either mode, you can choose a rewarded ad for one revive, with at most two rewarded revives per game or challenge attempt. The revive is granted only when the ad reports that the reward was earned.

Advertising privacy is managed through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), with required forms before an ad request is permitted. You can revisit available advertising privacy options in settings. A failed privacy update or unavailable ad does not prevent offline gameplay.

Google's advertising SDK can process IP addresses (including to estimate general location), ad interactions, diagnostic data and device identifiers, including advertising or app-set identifiers where permitted. Google uses these for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. This processing is separate from optional Analytics and Crashlytics; their switches do not disable AdMob's own processing.

Before release: Verify production ad-unit formats, final audience and age treatment, and the configured regional consent messages against the distributed build. Development test-operator confirmation is not player age verification.

6. Purposes and consent

Local data supports gameplay and restoration of progress. Optional statistics help improve the game, optional crash reports help diagnose failures, and advertising supports the game and provides chosen revive rewards. Support information is used to answer messages and handle requests.

Consent-dependent processing requires a valid choice. Reading a policy, continuing to play or enabling one optional service does not give blanket consent to other processing. Declining optional statistics or crash sharing does not prevent offline play.

Before release: Confirm the applicable legal bases, notices and consent requirements for the final audience and launch regions. This draft does not claim those release checks are complete.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's partner-app data explanation and Firebase privacy information. The final policy must describe any additional providers or processing introduced before release.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until updated or removed, including by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to Android backup and migration behavior. Clearing game storage resets progress. Turning off statistics resets local Analytics data without deleting saved-game progress.

Analytics retention is awaiting console verification. The applicable duration and reset-on-new-activity setting for The Cube's Firebase/Google Analytics property must be recorded here before this becomes the effective release policy. No property-specific retention duration has been verified for this draft.

Google documents a 90-day retention period for Crashlytics traces, extracted native-crash data and associated identifiers before removal from live and backup systems begins. Native minidumps are temporary processing data. This is the provider's documented practice, not a separately verified Analytics setting.

Analytics settings do not define retention for Crashlytics, AdMob data, standard aggregate reports, hosting logs or support correspondence. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Deleting local data or uninstalling the game does not automatically erase data already sent to a provider. There is no game account to delete. Without accounts or custom user IDs, an email address alone may not identify a particular app installation or Analytics record.
