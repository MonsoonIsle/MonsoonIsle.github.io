2. Local data and support

The game stores progress, scores, unfinished games, privacy choices and preferences on your device. Small local records help prevent duplicate gameplay events and duplicate ad rewards. It does not upload complete saved-game files to a developer-operated server or provide cloud saves. Android backup, migration and store services may have their own data practices.

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Optional usage analytics

The first-use privacy notice offers two separate switches, both initially off: Share usage statistics and Share crash diagnostics. You can save the choices you select or choose No thanks to decline both. We remember your saved response, including a refusal, and do not repeatedly ask you to enable sharing. You can change either choice later in settings.

The Android app includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Collection is off by default. If you enable Share usage statistics, Google's SDK can process app usage, an app-instance identifier, device and app information, and technical information such as language and approximate location derived from network information. See Google's Analytics data-collection information. We use this information to understand game use, difficulty, revives and ad reliability.

The current integration does not supply your name, email address, saved-game file or a custom user ID to Analytics. It disables Analytics advertising-ID collection and automatic screen reporting. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user data and ad-personalization consent remain denied independently of the statistics switch.

Gameplay statistics record the start and end of attempts, challenge completions, revives, and ad requests and outcomes. Depending on the event, they include the app version and build type, game mode, level, score, revive count and a randomly generated game-attempt identifier, plus the relevant outcome, ad format or standardized error code. The attempt identifier relates events within a game attempt; it is not an account or a developer-assigned persistent user ID. Separate IDs used to prevent counting an event twice stay on your device and are not sent as event parameters. Our gameplay events use a limited set of fields rather than free-text messages or saved-game contents. Google may also collect its documented automatic Analytics events. These identifiers can link related activity, so the data is not necessarily anonymous.

Test versions may also offer a statistics check that sends a test event only after you enable statistics.

Turning statistics off stops future collection and resets Analytics data on the device. That action does not automatically remove reports or records Google has already received.

4. Optional crash diagnostics

The Android app includes Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Share crash diagnostics is a separate choice and is off by default. It helps us investigate crashes and stability problems.

Reports can include crash time, stack traces and exception messages, app version, device and operating-system information, installation/session identifiers and native crash data. Diagnostic reports are not guaranteed to be anonymous. If usage statistics are also enabled, Analytics events may appear as diagnostic breadcrumbs. We do not intentionally attach saved games, names, email addresses or a custom user ID.

Crash reports can be stored on your device while sharing is disabled. The app requests deletion of unsent reports when it starts with sharing disabled and when you turn sharing off. Turning sharing off fully disables automatic uploads at the next app launch; it does not guarantee immediate deletion of every local report. Restart the app to fully apply this change. An upload already in progress or information already received by Google cannot be recalled by this switch.

See Firebase's crash-data description and Crashlytics' collection controls.

5. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob. Current test versions have commercial advertising disabled. Test advertising is available only on devices recognized as test devices by Google's advertising SDK and after the tester confirms they are at least 18 for that session. That confirmation is separate from advertising consent. Test requests and privacy updates can still send device and network information to Google.

Ads need a network connection and may be unavailable. There are no banner ads. When advertising is available in your version, the placements are:

  • Endless: At most one interstitial ad at an ordinary game-over. A final game-over within 60 seconds after a successful rewarded revive skips that interstitial.
  • Challenges: One interstitial opportunity after every three first-time level completions. Replaying a previously completed level does not advance this count.
  • Optional revives: In either mode, you can choose a rewarded ad for one revive, with at most two rewarded revives per game or challenge attempt. The revive is granted only when the ad reports that the reward was earned.

Advertising privacy choices are handled through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app checks the current privacy status and presents any required form before permitting an ad request. A request requires a successful privacy update and permission from UMP to request ads. You can review available advertising privacy options in settings. A failed privacy update or unavailable ad does not prevent offline gameplay.

For eligible ad testers in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, our European advertising privacy message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. Its partner or vendor list identifies the advertising partners and links to their privacy information. The choices and partners shown can change. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all ad-related processing; permitted non-personalized or limited ads can still use data for delivery, security and measurement.

Google and participating advertising partners may process IP addresses and inferred general location; app and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views; performance and diagnostic information; and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers relating to signed-in accounts on the device. Processing depends on the service, device settings and applicable privacy choices. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Ad data is used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and, where available and permitted, personalize ads. AdMob processing is separate from optional Analytics and Crashlytics; turning those switches off does not disable advertising processing. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad requests that reward and does not give consent to unrelated statistics, diagnostics or personalized advertising.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Local data supports gameplay and restoration of progress. Optional statistics help improve the game, optional crash reports help diagnose failures, and advertising supports the game and provides chosen revive rewards. Support information is used to answer messages and handle requests.

Consent-dependent processing requires a valid choice. Reading a policy, continuing to play or enabling one optional service does not give blanket consent to other processing. Declining optional statistics or crash sharing does not prevent offline play.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for optional usage statistics and crash-report sharing, and for advertising processing that requires consent. Processing needed to provide gameplay features or support you request is based on performing that service. Necessary security, abuse prevention and handling of correspondence may rely on our legitimate interests where permitted and not overridden by your rights. We rely on legal obligations for processing required by law. Legitimate interests do not replace legally required consent.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until updated or removed, including by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to Android backup and migration behavior. Clearing game storage resets progress. Turning off statistics resets the Analytics SDK's local data without deleting saved-game progress or the separate local records used to prevent duplicate events and rewards.

Google Analytics retention: Our property is configured to retain applicable event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. The setting to reset user data on new activity is enabled: new activity restarts the user identifier's retention period, so active users' identifiers can be retained for longer than 14 months from their first use. Expired data is deleted through Google's monthly deletion process. These controls do not apply to standard aggregate reports. See Google Analytics data retention.

Google documents a 90-day retention period for Crashlytics traces, extracted native-crash data and associated identifiers before removal from live and backup systems begins. Native minidumps are temporary processing data. See Firebase's retention information.

Analytics settings do not define retention for Crashlytics, AdMob data, standard aggregate reports, hosting logs or support correspondence. Google and advertising partners retain ad-related data under their policies for delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention and legal requirements; periods differ by data type. See Google's data-retention information. GitHub retains hosting information under its privacy notice. We keep support messages as needed to resolve requests, follow up and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them when no longer needed.

Deleting local data or uninstalling the game does not automatically erase data already sent to a provider. There is no game account to delete. Without accounts or custom user IDs, an email address alone may not identify a particular app installation or Analytics record.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

In-game and device controls

Open Settings → More Settings to reach About & Privacy, then choose Privacy & Data Choices. You can change the separate usage-statistics and crash-diagnostics switches and update or review available advertising privacy options. Restart after turning crash sharing off. Choose Privacy Policy to open this full policy, or Contact Support to email us. Offline gameplay remains available if you decline optional sharing or cannot connect.

Android or Google settings may let you reset or delete your advertising ID and manage advertising preferences. Available controls depend on the device and operating-system version. These actions do not erase data already received by providers or stop every form of ad-related processing.

Privacy requests and regional rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to know about and access your information, obtain a copy, correct or delete it, restrict processing, request portability, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

Right to object: Where applicable, you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Some jurisdictions also provide rights to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, and to appeal a privacy-request decision. Use the advertising privacy controls where available or contact us to exercise these rights. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can contact their local data-protection authority; UK users can contact the Information Commissioner's Office. Rights and exceptions depend on the law applicable to your request.

Send requests to mengjingchanyu@gmail.com, with the subject “The Cube Privacy Request” and enough context to identify your request.

We may need additional information to locate records or verify that a request concerns you. We will respond within applicable legal time limits and explain any exemption, identification limitation or provider-controlled processing that affects the request. An email address alone may not identify records associated with an installation identifier. Please do not send passwords or identity documents unsolicited.
