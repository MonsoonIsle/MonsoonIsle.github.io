1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Monsoon Isle (the developer of The Arrow, Android package com.monsoonisle.thearrow) handles information when you play The Arrow. The game does not require a player account.

2. Information on your device

The game stores settings, level progress, achievements, daily rewards, saved games and gameplay diagnostics on your device. A local analytics queue also stores gameplay events and randomly generated installation, session and event identifiers. These records support game features and analytics delivery.

3. Gameplay analytics

The Arrow integrates Google Analytics for Firebase to understand game use and improve gameplay and reliability. When analytics is enabled in the distributed build, gameplay and advertising events are sent to Google, including session duration, level starts and results, progress, hints, rewards, ad impressions and clicks, loading times and technical errors. Event data includes random installation and session identifiers, timestamps, game version, device model, operating system and language. Google’s SDK may also process app-instance identifiers and approximate geographic information. Our current Firebase configuration disables advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization signals for Analytics; this does not disable AdMob’s separate data processing.

4. Advertising

The Arrow uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial ads. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information such as performance and crash data. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ad personalization depends on applicable consent, SDK configuration and device settings. The game does not request precise device location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

5. Sharing and service providers

Analytics and advertising information is processed by Google through Firebase Analytics and AdMob. We use analytics reports to improve the game. We do not sell your local saved-game data. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond. Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. Your app store and operating system process information separately under their own policies.

6. Retention, security and deletion

Local saves remain until overwritten or removed. Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove local data; this resets progress. Removing local data does not automatically delete information previously received by Google. Google-hosted analytics is retained according to the Analytics property’s configured retention settings; advertising data is retained under Google’s policies. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request access or deletion where applicable; we may need information to locate records, and cannot identify all SDK records from a player name because the game has no account system. Google’s SDKs encrypt data in transit. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and children’s privacy

You can manage advertising privacy controls in Android and Google settings, where available, and remove local game data as described above. The current game has no in-game analytics opt-out switch. Applicable privacy rights are not limited by this policy. We do not ask children to provide their name or contact details within the game. Advertising and analytics SDKs may still process the technical data described above. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns can contact us.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this page and its date if our data practices change. Where required, material changes will be communicated and consent obtained before the relevant processing. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information below.

简体中文隐私政策

本政策适用范围

本隐私政策说明 The Arrow 的开发者 Monsoon Isle 如何处理玩家信息。Android 包名为 com.monsoonisle.thearrow。游戏不要求创建玩家账号。

设备上的本地信息

游戏在设备上保存设置、关卡进度、成就、每日奖励、未完成对局与游戏诊断记录。本地统计队列还保存游戏事件，以及随机生成的安装、会话和事件标识符，用于游戏功能和统计传输。

游戏使用统计

The Arrow 集成 Google Analytics for Firebase，用于了解游戏使用情况、改进玩法和稳定性。发行版本启用统计时，会向 Google 发送游戏与广告事件，包括会话时长、关卡开始和结果、进度、提示、奖励、广告展示与点击、加载时长和技术错误。事件数据包括随机安装与会话标识符、时间戳、游戏版本、设备型号、操作系统和语言。Google SDK 还可能处理应用实例标识符和大致地理信息。当前 Firebase 配置关闭统计服务的广告 ID 收集与广告个性化信号；这不代表 AdMob 的独立数据处理被关闭。

广告服务

The Arrow 使用 Google AdMob 提供激励广告和插屏广告。Google 移动广告 SDK 会自动收集和分享 IP 地址（可用于推断大致位置）、广告 ID 和应用集 ID 等设备或账号标识符、应用与广告交互，以及性能和崩溃等诊断信息。Google 将这些信息用于广告、分析和防欺诈。广告是否个性化取决于适用的同意状态、SDK 配置和设备设置。游戏不请求精确位置、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。

信息分享与服务提供商

统计和广告信息由 Google 通过 Firebase Analytics 与 AdMob 处理。我们使用统计报告改进游戏，不出售本地游戏存档。如果你联系我们，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱、消息等信息，并用于回复。服务提供商可能依据其适用条款和保障措施在你所在国家或地区之外处理数据。应用商店和操作系统依据各自政策独立处理信息。

保留、安全与删除

本地存档保留至被覆盖或删除。可以在 Android 设置中清除应用存储，或卸载应用来删除本地数据；这会重置进度。删除本地数据不会自动删除 Google 已接收的数据。Google 托管的统计数据按 Analytics 媒体资源配置的保留设置保存，广告数据按 Google 政策保留。支持通信仅在处理请求及履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。你可以联系我们提出适用的访问或删除请求；我们可能需要用于定位记录的信息。由于游戏没有账号系统，无法仅凭玩家名称识别所有 SDK 记录。Google SDK 对传输中的数据进行加密，但没有任何存储或传输方法能够保证绝对安全。

你的选择与儿童隐私

你可以通过 Android 和 Google 设置中可用的广告隐私选项管理相关偏好，并按上述方式删除本地游戏数据。当前游戏没有应用内统计关闭开关。本政策不限制适用的隐私权利。游戏不会要求儿童在游戏中提供姓名或联系方式，但广告和统计 SDK 仍可能处理上述技术数据。有隐私疑问的家长或监护人可以联系我们。

更新与联系

数据处理方式改变时，我们会更新本页面及日期。必要时会告知重大变更，并在相关处理开始前获得所需同意。隐私问题或请求请使用下方联系渠道。
